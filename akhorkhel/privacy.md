# Akhor Khel: Privacy Policy

**App:** Akhor Khel: Assamese Alphabet (Android, package `in.akhorkhel.app`)
**Developer:** Raman Kumar Kannaujiya
**Contact:** ramankumarkannaujiya@gmail.com
**Effective date:** 22 November 2026

Akhor Khel is a learning app for young children. It teaches them to see, trace and write Assamese and Bodo letters and numbers. This policy explains what information the app handles.

## 1. Information we collect

**We do not collect any personal information.** The app has no sign-up, no login and no server of its own.

- Child profiles hold only a nickname and an avatar that you choose. They are stored **only on your device** and are never sent to us.
- Learning progress (stars, attempts, settings) is stored **only on your device**. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
- Class progress reports leave the device only when **you** choose to share them using your phone's share menu.

## 2. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through **Google AdMob**. We have set AdMob up for apps directed at children:

- Ad requests are tagged as **child-directed**, and ads are limited to content rated **G (General Audiences)**.
- Ads are **not personalised**: no interest-based advertising and no remarketing.
- The app does **not** use or send the Android Advertising ID.
- A small banner appears only on menu screens (home, letter grid, word list, parent area). A full-screen ad may appear after a child finishes a set of letters; never when the app opens and never while the child is tracing or playing.
- No ads at all are shown before a parent has read the ads notice in the parent area, or during the first session after installing the app.
- There are no "watch an ad to unlock" (rewarded) ads.

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process limited technical information such as IP address (used for approximate location), device identifiers such as the app set ID (not the Advertising ID), app interactions with ads, and diagnostic data. Google handles this information under its own policies:

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 3. Children

The app is designed for children aged 3 to 8 and complies with the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no chat, no social features and no in-app purchases.

## 4. Permissions

The app uses internet access only to load ads. All lessons work without internet.

## 5. Your choices

- You can delete all data on the device (child profiles, progress and settings) at any time in the app: **Parent area → Delete all data**. Deleting a single child profile also deletes that child's progress.
- Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes everything too.
- You can stop ads from loading by using the app offline.

## 6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
